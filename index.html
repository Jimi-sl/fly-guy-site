How to Play

Ten seconds to learn. Forever to master.

Steer

Slide Fly Guy left and right through the gaps. The sky speeds up the longer you survive.

  • Phone Drag
  • Desktop ← → or drag

Blast

Shoot down drones for +100. Orange walls crack when you hit them. Grey walls don't, so go around.

  • Phone Tap
  • Desktop Click or Space

Grab

Hearts keep you flying. Gold coins refill your ammo. Miss them and you'll be sorry.

  • Tip Ammo runs out fast

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how Fly Guy ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile game Fly Guy ("the App").

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Display name — a name you choose to appear on the leaderboard. This does not need to be your real name.

Information Collected Automatically

  • Game data — your scores, enemies defeated, and games played, used to power the global leaderboard.
  • Device information — basic device data collected by our advertising partner Google AdMob, including advertising ID, device type, and OS version.

How We Use Your Information

  • To display your scores on the global leaderboard
  • To save your progress and high score locally on your device
  • To serve advertisements through Google AdMob
  • To improve the game experience

Advertising

Fly Guy uses Google AdMob to serve advertisements. AdMob may collect and use data to show you personalised ads based on your interests, including your device's advertising ID.

You can opt out of personalised advertising at any time:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalisation
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy → Tracking → disable for Fly Guy

For more information on how Google uses data, visit: policies.google.com/privacy

Data Storage

Your display name and game scores are stored securely on our servers hosted by Render (render.com). Your high score and settings are also stored locally on your device. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

Children's Privacy

Fly Guy is intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Data Retention

We retain your game data for as long as you use the app. You can request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

Your Rights

  • Access the data we hold about you
  • Request correction of your display name
  • Request deletion of your account and data

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the date at the top of this page.

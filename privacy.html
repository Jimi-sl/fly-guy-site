Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

The short version: you play as a guest, with no email or real name needed. We store your display name and scores so the leaderboard works. We count visits and game events with our own cookie-free analytics. We don't show ads right now, and we never sell your data.

This policy explains how Fly Guy ("we", "us"), an imgdoesit project, handles information when you visit flyguy.imgdoesit.com or play the Fly Guy browser game.

What we collect

Your guest account

The first time you open the game, it creates a guest account for you automatically. This account includes:

  • A random username and password. The game generates these; you never see them. We store the password only in scrambled (hashed) form.
  • Your display name. This is the name you choose, or one we pick for you, such as FlyGuy_1234. It doesn't need to be your real name, and we recommend it isn't.
  • Your game stats: scores, enemies defeated, how long each run lasted, how many games you've played, your best score, and when you created your account and last played.

We don't ask for your email, phone number, real name, age or location.

Analytics

We use Umami, an analytics tool we run on our own server, to understand how people find and play the game. It records:

  • Pages visited, the site that sent you (for example TikTok or Instagram), your browser, operating system, device type, screen size and country.
  • Game events: how long the game took to load, whether loading failed or was abandoned, when a run starts and ends, and the score, kills and length of each run.

Umami doesn't use cookies, doesn't store your IP address, and doesn't follow you across other websites. The data stays on our server and isn't shared with anyone.

Server logs

Like most websites, our game server keeps short technical logs of requests. These include IP addresses, and we use them to stop abuse, such as spam accounts or fake scores, and to fix problems. They're deleted automatically after about two weeks.

What's public

Your display name, best score and number of enemies defeated appear on the public leaderboard, in the game and on our homepage. Please don't use your real name or anything personal as your display name. We may remove or change names that are offensive.

What's stored in your browser

The game saves a few things in your browser's storage, not in cookies, so it remembers you next time:

  • A sign-in token that connects your browser to your guest account
  • Your display name, your best score on this device, and your settings
  • A copy of the game files, so it loads faster on later visits

If you clear your browser's site data, or play in a private window, the game starts a fresh guest account. We can't link your old one back to you automatically.

Services we rely on

  • GitHub Pages hosts this website. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Google Fonts supplies the fonts, so your browser connects to Google to download them. See Google's privacy policy.
  • The game server (accounts, scores and leaderboard) and our analytics run on a server we manage ourselves. Our hosting provider supplies the machine but doesn't use this data, and no other company processes it for us.

Advertising

Fly Guy doesn't currently show ads. If we add them in the future, for example so you can watch an ad to continue a run, we'll update this policy first. Where the law requires it, we'll ask for your consent before any ad-related cookies or tracking are used.

How we use your information

  • To run your guest account and save your scores
  • To show the leaderboard
  • To prevent cheating, spam and abuse
  • To understand what's working, such as load times and how long people play, and to improve the game

We never sell your information or share it for advertising.

How long we keep it

We keep your guest account and scores while the game is running, or until you ask us to delete them. Analytics data is kept in aggregate to track trends over time. Server logs are deleted after about two weeks.

Children

Fly Guy is for all ages, and you can play without giving us any personal information. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you think a child has put personal details in their display name, contact us and we'll remove them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under the UK and EU GDPR, you can ask us to:

  • Tell you what data we hold about you
  • Correct your display name, which you can also change yourself in the game's Settings
  • Delete your account and scores

Email us at the address below. Include your display name and roughly your best score, so we can find your account. Guest accounts aren't linked to an email address, so this helps us make sure we delete the right one. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle data, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
